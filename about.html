The Story

From the temples of Luxor
to the reefs of Marsa Alam

My name is Mohamed Hassan. I grew up in Luxor — a city that lives and breathes tourism — so understanding what makes a travel experience truly unforgettable has been part of my life since childhood.

More than five years ago I came to Marsa Alam, started working as an underwater photographer on snorkeling and diving trips, and never left. I earned my licensed snorkel guide certification here, and spent years documenting the reefs, the dolphins, the dugongs, and every hidden corner of this coastline through my lens.

Most visitors to Egypt head straight to Cairo, Luxor, Aswan — or if they want the sea, Sharm El Sheikh or Hurghada. Marsa Alam stays off the radar. That's exactly what makes it special — and exactly why I built Beyond The Shore. This place deserves to be seen by the world.

Why Here

The Red Sea's best-kept secret

Marsa Alam offers experiences you simply cannot find anywhere else in Egypt. Wild dolphins at Sataya. Dugongs at Abu Dabbab. Three uninhabited islands at Hamata. Sea turtles that greet you at the surface. The reefs here are pristine, the water is clear year-round, and the groups are small.

My background in photography means I know every reef, every current, and exactly where to be at the right moment — so your experience isn't just a trip, it's something you'll want to remember forever.
