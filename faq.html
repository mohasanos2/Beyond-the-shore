Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know before your Red Sea adventure.

🌊 About the Trips
What's included in the trip price?
Equipment, a professional guide, lunch, and hotel transfer are typically included — though inclusions vary per trip. Check the individual trip page for the full breakdown before booking.
Do I need swimming experience?
Basic swimming ability is recommended for most water activities. Life jackets are always provided, and our guides are trained to support all ability levels. If you're unsure, just ask us before booking.
What should I bring?
We recommend: reef-safe sunscreen, a towel, a change of clothes, and some cash for park entry fees (where applicable). For snorkeling trips, we supply the gear — just bring yourself!
Are the trips suitable for children?
Most of our trips welcome children. Minimum age varies by trip — check the specific trip page. For family-specific queries or children under 6, please contact us on WhatsApp first so we can advise the best option.
What if the weather is bad?
We monitor sea and weather conditions daily. If conditions are unsafe we will contact you to reschedule to a date that suits you, or issue a full refund — no questions asked.
📋 Booking & Payment
How do I confirm my booking?
Submit the booking form on our website — no payment is needed upfront. We'll review your request and contact you within 24 hours via your preferred method (WhatsApp or email) to confirm availability.
Do I pay online?
No online payment is required at the time of booking. Payment is arranged directly after confirmation — we accept cash or bank transfer. We'll explain all options when we get in touch.
Can I cancel or reschedule?
Yes. You can cancel or reschedule up to 48 hours before your trip start time for a full refund. For cancellations within 48 hours, please contact us directly on WhatsApp and we'll do our best to help.
Can I book for a group?
Absolutely — we love group bookings! Contact us directly on WhatsApp for group rates and to discuss private trip options tailored to your group's needs.
📍 Logistics
Where do the trips depart from?
Departure point is specified on each trip page — typically Hamata Marina, Marsa Alam Marina, or Port Ghalib. We'll also confirm the exact meeting point when we contact you after booking.
Is hotel pickup included?
Hotel pickup is included for most Marsa Alam area trips. Please confirm when booking as availability depends on your hotel location and the specific trip.
What languages do the guides speak?
Our guides speak English, Arabic, Russian, and German. Language availability varies by trip and guide — let us know your preference when booking and we'll do our best to match you.
⭐ General
Are you a licensed operator?
Yes. Beyond The Shore is a fully licensed Egyptian tour operator. We operate in compliance with all local regulations and tourism authority requirements.
How do I contact you urgently?
The fastest way to reach us is WhatsApp: +20 103 742 0949. We typically respond within minutes during operating hours.
Do you operate year round?
Yes — the Red Sea's consistently warm and clear waters make it one of the few destinations where trips run every month of the year. Some experiences are seasonal, so check individual trip pages for availability.
